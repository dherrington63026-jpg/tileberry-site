Tileberry Support

Need help? Email support@kelbrasolutions.net and we will reply as soon as we can. It helps to tell us your phone model and what happened.

How do I play?

Tap two matching tiles to remove them. A tile is free when nothing is on top of it and its left or right side is open. Bright tiles are free, dim tiles are boxed in. Clear every tile to win.

I have no matches left

Tap Shuffle. It is always free, and the new arrangement can always be finished.

The tiles are small

Tap the magnifier button at the top of the game to turn Big tiles on. It uses smaller boards so each tile is larger.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own device. If you delete the app or clear its data, progress is removed. Playing in a browser and in the app are separate. We do not use accounts.

Restoring purchases

If you bought something and it is missing (for example after getting a new phone), open the shop settings and tap Restore purchases. Purchases belong to your Apple ID or Google account.

Refunds and subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. To get a refund or cancel the Tileberry Club subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Why are there no ads?

Because nobody likes them. Tileberry is free, and we are supported by people who choose optional extras such as scenery, tile styles and music.
